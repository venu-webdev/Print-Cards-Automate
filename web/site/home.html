FAQ

Questions shops ask

Do I need Photoshop or design skills?

No. Card Print Studio does the cropping, cleaning and sizing that you would otherwise do by hand in Photoshop. You only check the preview and print.

Which printers does it work with?

PVC cards print directly on the Epson L8050 (and other Epson printers with a Disc/ID card tray). A4 laminated sheets print on any Windows printer at true 100% size. You can always save JPEGs and print from your usual software.

Are my customers’ documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your shop computer. The only online step is the rice card lookup, which reads the public AePDS page for the RC number you enter.

What about password-protected PDFs?

e-Aadhaar and e-PAN files that use the standard file-name password are opened automatically. For anything else you can type the password once.

Is this an official government product?

No. Card Print Studio is an independent tool for printing copies of documents that customers already have. It is not affiliated with UIDAI, the Income Tax Department, the Election Commission, transport departments or the Civil Supplies Department.
